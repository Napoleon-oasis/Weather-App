# Weather-App
🌤️ Weather Forecast — Your Daily Weather Guide

Stay ahead of the weather and make every day count! ☀️ Get the latest weather updates and forecasts to help you plan your day with confidence. From sunny skies 🌞 to cloudy afternoons ☁️ and unexpected rain showers 🌧️, stay informed wherever you go.

## 🌦️ Key Features

- ☀️ **Current Weather:** Check the current temperature, weather conditions, and feels-like temperature.
- 🌧️ **Chance of Rain:** Discover the likelihood of rainfall and prepare for possible showers.
- 🌡️ **Temperature Forecast:** View daily high and low temperatures and upcoming changes.
- ☁️ **Weather Conditions:** Stay updated on sunny, cloudy, rainy, and stormy weather.
- 💧 **Humidity:** Monitor moisture levels in the air and understand their impact on comfort.
- 💨 **Wind Speed:** Check wind speed and direction before heading outdoors.
- 🕶️ **UV Index:** Track UV levels and know when sun protection is recommended.
- 🕒 **Hourly Forecast:** See how weather conditions may change throughout the day.
- 📅 **7-Day Forecast:** Plan your activities with upcoming weekly predictions.
- ⚠️ **Weather Alerts:** Stay informed about severe weather warnings when available.
